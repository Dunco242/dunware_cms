Your Personalized Onboarding Journey

We've designed a step-by-step onboarding process to help you get the most out of DunWare CRM. Our goal is to ensure you're successful with our platform from day one.

1
Set Up Your Account

The first step is setting up your account with all the necessary information. This helps personalize your CRM experience.

  • Complete your profile information
  • Configure basic settings
  • Set up team members (if applicable)
2
Import Your Data

Bring your existing customer data into DunWare CRM to get up and running quickly. We support imports from CSV, Excel, and other popular CRM systems.

  • Import customers and contacts
  • Import leads and opportunities
  • Import products or services
3
Learn Key Features

Explore the core features of DunWare CRM through guided tutorials and walkthroughs designed to help you understand the platform.

  • Customer management
  • Lead tracking and conversion
  • Task and meeting scheduling
  • Projects and document management
4
Start Using DunWare CRM

Apply what you've learned and start using DunWare CRM for your daily business operations. We'll be there to support you every step of the way.

  • Create your first customer record
  • Schedule and track customer interactions
  • Generate your first reports
  • Set up customized workflows

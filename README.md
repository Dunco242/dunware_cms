# DunWare - CMS

Offering a tailored CRM (Customer Relationship Management System), for the new start-up company.
When starting your business, the overhead is already astronomical, from licenses,
permits, certification, rent and the like; a new start-up does not want to have to
come up with another recurring expense. This is where we step in to assist.

- Need your employee data retained in one place?
- Need to assign certain roles for your staff using your system?
- Need your customer information in one easy to access system?
- Need to create quotes and invoices and keep them on record?

How about having all these services, and more all in one application,
one that belongs to your company, bearing your company logo? With this
option, there is no need for a subscription based CRM.

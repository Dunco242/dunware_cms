Your Privacy Rights

At DunWare Solutions, we respect your privacy rights and are committed to complying with applicable data protection laws including:

  • General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)
  • California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA)
  • Other state and international privacy regulations

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access personal information we have about you
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Obtain a copy of your data in a portable format

We will respond to your request within the timeframe required by applicable law (typically 30 days).
